## Implementation

Existing production proved nine new image-anchored takes, private delivery, timeline return, comparison/replacement/undo, hosted cloud save/restore and a real browser export. The new SDK read/download matched a real completed take; new SDK submissions are contract-tested with fixtures, not yet paid-live tested.

Two editable recipes are implemented: creator/product reels and cinematic sizzles. References and titles stay on separate tracks, and local editing needs no subscription. Public paid generation and checkout remain gated. A fixture entitlement is not proof of payment.
